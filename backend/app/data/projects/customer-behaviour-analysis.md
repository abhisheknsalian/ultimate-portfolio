# Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

## Project Summary

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behaviour to uncover purchasing patterns, spending trends, and actionable business insights. Using data analytics techniques, I explored customer transactions to understand buying habits, identify valuable customer segments, and support data-driven business decisions.

The project demonstrates practical experience in exploratory data analysis (EDA), SQL, business intelligence, and dashboard development using Power BI.

---

# Project Overview

Understanding customer behaviour is essential for businesses aiming to improve marketing strategies, inventory planning, and customer retention.

In this project, I analyzed shopping data to identify purchasing trends, customer preferences, and revenue-driving segments. The findings can help businesses optimize product offerings, improve customer targeting, and make informed strategic decisions.

---

# Why I Built This Project

I wanted to strengthen my data analytics skills by working with customer transaction data and solving real-world business problems.

The project allowed me to practice data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL querying, visualization, and dashboard development while learning how analytics can support business decision-making.

---

# Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze customer purchase behaviour
- Identify high-value customer segments
- Examine product category performance
- Study seasonal and monthly purchasing trends
- Understand customer purchase frequency
- Generate actionable business insights
- Build an interactive business dashboard

---

# Data Analysis Performed

The analysis included:

## Customer Segmentation

Customers were grouped based on purchasing behaviour and spending patterns to identify high-value customer segments.

---

## Revenue Analysis

Revenue distribution was analyzed to determine which customers and product categories generated the highest sales.

---

## Product Category Analysis

The project identified the best-performing product categories and compared their contribution to overall revenue.

---

## Sales Trend Analysis

Monthly and seasonal sales trends were analyzed to understand demand fluctuations and peak shopping periods.

---

## Customer Purchase Frequency

Customer purchasing frequency was examined to identify repeat buyers and evaluate customer loyalty.

---

## SQL Business Queries

SQL was used to answer business questions related to customer behaviour, product performance, and sales metrics.

---

## Interactive Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to visualize business insights and allow users to explore the data dynamically.

---

# Key Insights

The analysis revealed several important business insights:

- High-value customer segments contributed the majority of overall revenue.
- Certain product categories consistently outperformed others.
- Peak shopping periods were identified through seasonal trend analysis.
- Repeat customers generated a significant portion of sales.
- Data visualization helped communicate complex business insights effectively.
